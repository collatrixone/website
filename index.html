FAQs

Questions, answered

What is a loan against securities?

A secured loan where you pledge investments such as mutual funds, shares or bonds as collateral. You keep ownership while a lien is marked in favour of the lender.

How much can I borrow?

The limit depends on the type and quality of collateral and lender policy. Equity collateral typically supports a lower percentage than debt instruments. Use the eligibility checker for an indicative figure.

Do I lose my dividends or returns?

No. You continue to own the securities. Corporate benefits and returns remain with you, subject to the pledge terms.

What happens if my portfolio value falls?

If the collateral value drops below the required cover, you may be asked to add securities or part-repay. We alert you early so you can act.

Are there prepayment charges?

Charges, if any, depend on the lender and product and are shown in your key fact statement before you sign.

How long does disbursal take?

The process is fully digital. Timelines depend on pledge confirmation and verification completing; most steps are done online in a single session.
